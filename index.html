Methodology & caveats

  1. Figures are the actual consolidated FY24 balance sheet, P&L, and cash flow line items as filed by each company — extracted directly from their published financial statements, not estimated.
  2. "COGS" doesn't map cleanly onto a services company: Infosys reports no cost-of-materials line, so its gross margin here approximates cost of revenue as employee costs + subcontractor costs. Treat Infosys's gross margin as directionally indicative, not a like-for-like figure against Tata's.
  3. Peer-relative anomaly detection (z-scores) is intentionally not shown — it requires 3 or more companies to be statistically meaningful and silently misfires with just two.
  4. This is a mechanics demonstration, not investment research. No forward-looking claims are made about either company.
