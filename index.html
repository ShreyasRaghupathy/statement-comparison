Methodology & caveats

  1. Figures are the actual consolidated FY24 balance sheet and P&L line items as filed by each company — pulled directly from their published financials, not estimated.
  2. Mahindra & Mahindra's consolidated filing folds in Mahindra & Mahindra Financial Services, an NBFC lending arm. That's why its current assets, current liabilities, and total debt look unusually large next to a "pure" auto manufacturer like Maruti — the current ratio and debt-to-equity here reflect a manufacturer-plus-lender group, not an apples-to-apples plant comparison.
  3. "COGS" is approximated as total reported operating expenses for both companies (neither publishes a clean, directly comparable cost-of-materials-only line at the consolidated level), so gross and operating margin read identically here — treat them as one combined profitability read rather than two distinct figures.
  4. Peer-relative anomaly detection (z-scores) is intentionally not shown — it requires 3 or more companies to be statistically meaningful and silently misfires with just two.
  5. This is a mechanics demonstration, not investment research. No forward-looking claims are made about either company.
